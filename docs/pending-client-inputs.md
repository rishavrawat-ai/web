# Client inputs to finalize the Workhour Business website

The review website now contains the complete page structure and the copy supplied in the three DOCX files. These items need client confirmation or production access before the site can be published as a finished service.

## Domains and deployment

- Confirm the main domain and hosting account.
- Confirm whether all seven brands should use subdomains under the main domain. Proposed pattern: `inceptura.workhourbusiness.com`, `employaa.workhourbusiness.com`, `remunerax.workhourbusiness.com`, `thinkrunspace.workhourbusiness.com`, `leaderspinnacle.workhourbusiness.com`, `elevatrain.workhourbusiness.com`, and `gotravelglobal.workhourbusiness.com`.
- Confirm DNS/registrar access and whether each subdomain should show its own website or a brand page managed by the main site. The DOCX examples currently mix subdomain, path, and separate-domain URLs.

## Brand and page content

- Seven brand logos, brand banner/background images, and approved banner quotes.
- Awards and certifications, including names, dates, issuing organizations, and approved images.
- Final IGNITA announcement copy, date, image, and destination URL.
- Mindfulness podcast and ARC/SNU event images, descriptions, dates, and destination URLs.
- Contact address, phone, enquiry email/recipient, and approval of the form fields and privacy text.
- Footer social profile URLs, legal links, copyright wording, and newsletter platform/list destination.
- One verified list of operating offices and client centres. The About Us and Global Presence sections give conflicting regional lists.
- Final choices for “Endurance or Resilience” and “Influence or Legacy,” and identification of the unnamed homepage sections to remove/keep.

## CMS and production connection

- Production database connection string and Payload secret/configuration for the existing `workhour-web` CMS.
- Confirm who will manage the CMS and which content needs approval before publishing.
- Connect the review website content to the CMS, then configure the main domain and brand subdomains for deployment.

## Claims for client verification

- Verify the supplied company history, locations, statistics, client outcomes, future targets, and other business claims before publication. The website preserves the client-provided wording; the build does not independently validate those claims.
