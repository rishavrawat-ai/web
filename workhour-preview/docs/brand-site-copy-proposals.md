# Brand service copy for client review

The service descriptions in `data/brand-service-content.ts` were written for this frontend from the supplied service titles and business descriptions. They are proposals pending client approval. The public pages present the copy without an approval banner; this note remains internal.

Every listed service has a distinct overview, intended audience, service scope, approach, and practical benefits. No unprovided delivery guarantee, office location, credential, client relationship, or service result has been added. Jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, travel, and compliance work is described as requiring the appropriate client or professional review.

`Employee Self Service Portal` is described as a service concept only. No portal, login, employee records, or working functionality is implemented.
